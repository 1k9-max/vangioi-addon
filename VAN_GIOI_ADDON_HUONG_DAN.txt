1. MOD BAT BUOC
----------------

Neu thieu Meteor Client, addon se khong load duoc.

2. MOD KHUYEN NGHI / TUY CHON
------------------------------
- Litematica: can cho module Fly To Placement.
- MaLiLib: dependency cua Litematica, nen cai cung Litematica.
- Litematica Printer: tuy chon, dung chung voi Fly To Placement de tu dong dat block.
- Fabric API: tuy modpack co the da co san.

Neu khong cai Litematica hoac MaLiLib:
- Van Gioi Addon van khoi dong binh thuong.
- Cac module khac van dung duoc.
- Chi module Fly To Placement khong co tac dung vi khong co schematic Litematica.

3. CAI DAT
----------
1. Cai Fabric Loader cho Minecraft 1.21.4.
2. Cai Java 21.
3. Cai Meteor Client dung ban 1.21.4.
4. Chep van-gioi-addon-0.1.0.jar vao thu muc .minecraft/mods.
5. Neu dung Fly To Placement, chep Litematica va MaLiLib vao mods.
6. Mo game, vao Meteor -> Modules -> Van Gioi Addon.

4. DANH SACH MODULE
-------------------
Auto Crop Farmer
- Chon Item 1: item lam cay thay doi.
- Chon Item 2: item dung de trong.
- Chon Pos 1 va Pos 2 de tao vung.
- Co auto trong, giam sat va tuoi cay.
- Co refill Linh Dich theo Smart hoac mode thuong.
- Command reset: .clear-farmer hoac .reset-farmer.

Auto Crop Waterer
- Tu dong tim vi tri can tuoi.
- Tuoi theo khoang cach va delay da cai.
- Ho tro di chuyen Normal, Fly hoac Goto.
- Co refill Linh Dich.

Auto Boss
- weapon-slot: slot vu khi.
- tool-slot: slot Truyen Tong Lenh.
- difficulty: EASY, MEDIUM, HARD.
- farm-mode: QUET hoac CLEAR.
- attack-mode: SINGLE hoac COMBO.
- click-mode: LEFT hoac RIGHT khi dung SINGLE.
- click-delay-ticks: delay giua cac lan click.
- gui-open-delay-ticks: delay sau khi GUI hien.
- tool-select-delay-ticks: delay sau khi chon Truyen Tong Lenh.
- gui-retry-after-ticks: thoi gian thu mo lai GUI.
- gui-delay-ticks: delay giua cac lan click trong GUI.
- travel-seconds: thoi gian cho qua boss.
- extend-travel-if-not-ready: gia han neu chua chon xong GUI.
- travel-extend-max-ticks: gioi han gia han.
- fight-seconds: thoi gian danh khi SINGLE.
- combo-repeat-count: so chu ky khi COMBO.
- post-fight-delay-ticks: delay sau khi danh xong.
- debug-log, debug-chat-echo, debug-include-tick-spam: debug.

Auto Dotpha
- item-slot: slot item do kiep, tu 1 den 9.
- item-delay-ticks: delay truoc khi dung item.
- command-delay-ticks: delay giua /dotpha va /dokiep.
- item: mo Edit, bat dung 1 item custom.
- refill-amount: neu item trong slot it hon muc nay thi refill.
- Bat module la tu dong gui /dotpha.
- Khi server yeu cau do kiep, module chuyen slot, dung item, chuyen lai slot cu.
- Neu slot co item khac, module dua item do ra o trong roi nap item dung.
- Neu inventory khong du item, module dung phan con lai va tu tat khi het.

Auto Phoban
- target: NGUC_THAN, TAN_TICH, DAU_TRUONG.
- scan-delay-ticks: delay quet GUI.
- tool-slot: slot item mo GUI pho ban.
- right-click-delay-ticks: delay truoc chuot phai.
- open-retry-delay-ticks: delay thu mo lai GUI.
- command-open-delay-ticks: delay goi lai /phoban neu slot trong.
- F4: doi target pho ban.

Auto Fish
- rod-slot: slot can cau.
- bait-slot: slot moi.
- auto-rebait: tu dong gan moi.
- auto-switch-rod: tu dong doi can sap hong.
- auto-recast: tu dong quang can lai.
- Co doc ActionBar va chat minigame.

Auto Furnace
- Quet cac lo trong vung da chon.
- Chon truc X/Z, toc do bay, delay mo lo va timeout Baritone.
- Ho tro GOTO hoac FLY.

Auto Farm
- Tu dong thu hoach va trong lai.
- radius: ban kinh quet.
- harvest-delay / plant-delay: delay thao tac.
- Danh sach cay chin va block nen co the cau hinh.

Auto Drop Vanilla
- inventory: drop item trong inventory.
- chi-hotbar: chi drop item hotbar.
- gui-ruong-hostbar: drop item trong GUI container.
- tick-delay: delay quet.
- items: list item vanilla.
- custom-items: list item custom, bam Edit de them/xoa/bat/tat.

Auto Accept
- title-contains: tu khoa tieu de GUI.
- item-contains: tu khoa item can click.

Auto Minigame
- Doc CustomModelData va click slot muc tieu.
- Cai theo setting cua module trong Meteor.

Alchemical Pill Making
- Chon cac mau stained glass pane se tu dong drop.
- Co setting bo qua hotbar va delay thao tac.

Chat Auto Responder
- Tao profile tra loi chat.
- Co the bat/tat tung profile trong GUI module.

Fly To Placement
- Can Litematica + MaLiLib.
- Can load schematic va bat overlay Litematica.
- speed: toc do bay.
- search-radius: ban kinh tim block thieu.
- hover-height: do cao bay tren block.
- Neu khong co Litematica, module se canh bao va khong lam gi.

Fly Goto
- Bay thang den toa do X/Y/Z.
- Co speed, delay va timeout.

Auto Walk Straight
- Di thang theo huong dang nhin.
- Co dieu chinh input va gioi han drift.

Linh Thao Locations
- Hien ten Linh Thao lay tu lenh /linhthao tren world.
- Co setting mau va kich thuoc chu.

Chest Drop All Button
- Them nut Drop All vao GUI ruong.
- Co danh sach loai GUI va delay giua cac lan vut.

GUI Dumper
- Bat module de hien nut Dump GUI trong GUI container.
- Luu tai: gui_dumps/[ip-server]/[ten-gui]/file.json.
- Dump tiep cung GUI tao file1.json, file2.json...

Experience HUD
- HUD kinh nghiem, dang ky trong Meteor HUD.
